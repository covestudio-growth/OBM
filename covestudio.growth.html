Questions

Common questions

I've only just started. Is this for me?

Yes. Many of our clients are in their first year. We start with what matters most at your stage, which is usually getting known and winning your first clients, then build the systems as you grow.

How much does it cost?

Essentials starts from USD 750 a month. Growth starts from USD 2,600 for the one-off setup, then from USD 1,500 a month once your systems are live. Launch Setup starts from USD 3,000, one-off. Partner is priced to fit your business after a short call. Australian clients can pay in AUD.

How long does setup take?

Most setups take 3 to 6 weeks. We'll give you a timeline before we start.

Do I pay for the apps separately?

Yes. Apps like booking and email tools are paid directly by you, in your name. Many have free or low-cost plans for new businesses, and we'll help you choose options that fit your budget.

If we stop working together, do I keep my systems?

Yes. Everything is set up in your own accounts, so it all stays with you. We also give you simple written guides for how it works.

Do I need to change the tools I already use?

No. We work with the tools you have where we can. If something isn't working well, we'll suggest a simpler option.

Do you work with clients outside Australia?

Yes. Our main clients are in Australia, and we work with service businesses in other countries too.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Launch Setup is one-off, with nothing ongoing. For monthly plans, we ask for 3 months to start, because systems and marketing need a little time to show results. After that, you can change or stop with notice.
